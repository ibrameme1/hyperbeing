# Quality reference library — the team's ideal slide prompts

These are the team's gold-standard single-image slide prompts (verbatim). They
define the QUALITY BAR and the creative register: concept, specificity,
copywriting voice, cultural detail, layout density, typography and finish.
Layered slides must look at least this good. They are REFERENCES ONLY: never
copy their brands, products, places, statistics or copy into a user's deck.

How to read them as layers: every quoted word becomes native text; cards,
pills, strips, stat rows and tables become native shapes + text; charts become
native charts; the hero object and its companions become one "cluster"; a
photographic scene becomes the background; glows/aurora/haze live inside the
background image.
