FAQ

## Questions, *answered.*

What does HyperBeing do?

HyperBeing is an AI presentation maker that builds fully designed decks from a written brief. Nova, its AI art director, reads the entire brief, proposes a slide plan, then composes every slide from scratch: generated imagery plus real, editable text, charts and shapes, instead of filling in a template.

How is it different from Gamma or Canva?

It holds large context. You can hand it a long, messy brief, PDFs, spreadsheets or an old deck, and it keeps the full thread instead of collapsing it into a generic layout. It also adapts the deck's style to your industry and audience, so a fintech board update and a fashion launch don't come out looking the same.

How much does HyperBeing cost?

Plans range from $25/month (Basic) to $299/month (Ultra 4) on monthly billing. Annual billing lowers the cost, from $20/month for Basic to $209/month for Ultra 4. New accounts get 54 free credits, enough for a first 3-slide deck, with no card required.

How long does a deck take?

A typical deck takes 5 to 10 minutes from brief to finished slides, depending on length and detail. None of that time is manual design work.

What's the difference between Ultra 1 and Ultra 4?

Only the monthly credits: 8,000, 11,200, 14,400 or 16,000. The bigger the credit level, the bigger the annual discount, from 22% up to 30%.

What can I export to?

PowerPoint (.pptx) with native, editable text boxes, charts and pictures, plus PDF and PNG. Exporting is always free.

Can I edit the slides after they are generated?

Yes. Text, shapes, charts and icons are real objects you can edit directly in HyperBeing or in PowerPoint after export. You can also ask Nova to change a slide in plain language.

Do I need design experience?

No. You write or paste the brief, Nova builds the deck.

Do you serve companies outside the United States?

Yes. HyperBeing is a web app available worldwide, with no location restrictions.
